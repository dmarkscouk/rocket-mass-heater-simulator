      About the model

      A lumped thermal model on a 25 cm grid with 15 cm layers. The fire heats the riser liner; combustion is cleaner as the liner passes ~450–850 °C. The barrel radiates to the room from whatever part of it is left bare. Blocks built against it are cut to fit round it and take heat from it in proportion to their conductivity, so insulation keeps the drum hot (which also burns off leftover smoke) and sends the heat on down the flue. Flue gas then gives heat to each mass cell it passes through, and the mass conducts it to the bench surface, the floor and its neighbours. Draft is judged against rule-of-thumb flue lengths (about 7.5 m for 6″, 12 m for 8″, plus 1.5 m per bend). Results are for comparing designs, not for sizing a build.
